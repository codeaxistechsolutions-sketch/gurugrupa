About Student Project Portal

Student Project Portal is an online platform designed to simplify and organize the complete student project registration and management process. Students can register, submit their academic and project information, upload required documents, receive quotations, make payments and track their project progress through a single portal.


The system provides a structured workflow from initial registration to final project delivery. It helps students and project management teams maintain project-related information in an organized and transparent manner.

What We Provide
Student Registration

Students can create their account and submit personal and academic information online.

Project Management

Students can submit project details, requirements, technology preferences and project information.

Document Upload

Required college guidelines, project references and academic documents can be uploaded securely.

Quotation

Students can view project quotations and accept the quotation before proceeding.

Payment

Payment details can be managed and students can view their payment status and receipt.

Project Tracking

Students can track their application and project development progress online.

Project Development Support

The portal supports students throughout the complete project lifecycle. After registration and submission of project details, the project can move through different stages such as development, testing, documentation and final delivery.

Our goal is to provide students with a simple, transparent and organized platform for project registration, development and management.
